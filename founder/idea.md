# Consent management platform on BlockDB (working name not decided)

- What it is: a consent management platform that records and proves consent, using BlockDB (an open-source, append-only, hash-chained database) as its storage engine.
- Who it is for: Indian companies (data fiduciaries) that must comply with the DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025; the buyer is their privacy/compliance team. Sector not decided yet.
- What it sells, at what price: the consent platform as a managed cloud service. Price not decided yet.
- Where and how: India, online. The marketing hook is the DPDP compliance deadline (secondary sources put the main obligations at 13 May 2027; verify against official MeitY text). Channel not decided yet. BlockDB itself stays a separate, reusable engine that can serve other use cases later.
- Budget and constraints: small team, bootstrapped (no outside funding). No budget figure given.
